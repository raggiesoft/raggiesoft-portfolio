DRY Information Architecture & Subdomain Topology *.raggiesoft.com + michaelpragsdale.com

How 17 Web Properties Stay DRY, Fast & Maintainable

  • DRY Role Separation Across Hubs: Rather than duplicating 17-card lists across sites, raggiesoft.com acts as a focused front-door homepage, waystation.raggiesoft.com serves as both the single canonical Global Directory and the wildcard *.raggiesoft.com HTTP 404 safety net, jessica.raggiesoft.com documents the personal & creative story behind each project, launcher.raggiesoft.com unites the consumer desktop suite, and a11y.raggiesoft.com presents our enterprise WCAG & Section 508 IDE.
  • Right-Sized Consolidation vs. Decoupling: Multi-artist catalogs share a single Astro 6 codebase (engineroom.raggiesoft.com/artists/{slug}), while stateful audio playback & WebVTT captioning run in Svelte 5 (music.raggiesoft.com) and cross-origin static JSON APIs are served from backstage.raggiesoft.com to both web and native C#/.NET 10 WinForms/WPF, C++20, and SwiftUI apps.
  • Shared WCAG 2.1 AA Navigation Layer: A lightweight shared script and stylesheet (raggiesoft-network.js / .css) provides keyboard-accessible navigation across all 17 properties and integrates cleanly into mobile disclosure menus.
